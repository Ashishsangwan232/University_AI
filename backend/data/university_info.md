## About LPU
- Full name: Lovely Professional University (LPU), a private university in Punjab, India.
- Established 2005. Its parent body, Lovely International Trust, started a business and IT school in Phagwara in 2001.
- UGC recognition received in 2006; has UGC Graded Autonomy.
- NAAC grade A++ (score 3.68 out of 4, awarded 2023).
- NIRF 2025: 31st among government and private universities in India.
- Offers 150+ programmes across about 27 disciplines, with 200+ industry collaborations.
- Around 30,000-35,000 students on campus from 50+ countries (figures differ across the site).
- Leadership: Founder Chancellor Dr. Ashok Kumar Mittal; Pro-Chancellor Dr. Rashmi Mittal; Vice Chancellor Prof. Dr. Jaspal Singh Sandhu; Pro Vice Chancellor Dr. Lovi Raj Gupta; Executive Dean & Registrar Dr. Monica Gulati.
Source: https://www.lpu.in/about-lpu/

## Attendance rules (minimum attendance, 75%)
- Students should attend 80% or more, in aggregate, of delivered classes across all registered courses of the term (theory, tutorials, practicals, projects, training, electives and backlog courses).
- A 5% relaxation covers medical reasons and other genuine emergencies, so the hard minimum is 75% aggregate attendance.
- Students who join late get no extra relaxation for the classes missed.
- A student declared ineligible for exams because of attendance shortage may have to vacate the hostel, and low attendance or CGPA can bar hostel allotment next term.
- An older admissions guideline also mentioned a 65% minimum in each individual course; the 2026 prospectus does not repeat it, so confirm with your programme office.
Source: LPU Prospectus 2026, Part C, "Attendance Conditions" - https://www.lpu.in/lpu-assets/download/prospectus/guidelines-and-application-forms-part-c.pdf

## Hostel (residence) rooms and facilities
- Separate hostels for boys and girls. Room types: standard rooms (air-cooled or AC, 1 to 8 seater), AC studio apartments (1 to 4 seater, some with kitchenette), and dormitories.
- Each room: attached washroom, bed, mattress, table, chair, almirah, cooler or AC as opted.
- Geyser hot water in winter about two hours a day per room; power backup for lights only (not AC, cooler or geyser).
- Wi-Fi at scheduled hours for academic use; 24x7 security, CCTV, fire safety.
- Entry to hostels is through turnstile gates with biometric records.
- Allowed appliances: kettle, iron, steamer, trimmer, hair dryer, straightener (extra electricity billed).
- Electricity beyond the free units is charged at actual cost and split equally among roommates.
- Stay covers one session (about 10 months, excluding vacations). Join up to two days before the session and vacate within two days after the regular exams. Staying later (summer school, reappear) costs extra.
- Rooms normally cannot be changed during the session. Allotment is first come, first served.
- The university may inspect rooms and belongings at any time. Visitors and parents need the warden's permission.
- Parents can be informed of leave/movement through the online leave system (SMS).
Source: https://www.lpu.in/student-services/residence.php and Prospectus 2026 Part C

## Hostel leave application (how to apply)
- Hostel leave is applied for in UMS, on the "Hostel Leave Application" page (UMS page frmStudentHostelLeaveApplicationTermWise.aspx). CampusAI can show your balance and check a draft but cannot submit for you.
- Check your balance first: there are day leaves and night leaves, each with a total, used and remaining count for the term.
- The form asks for five things: Leave Type (the types offered for your term), Start Date & Time of Leave, Leave Ending Date Time, Visit place, and Reason of Leaving.
- Dates and times are entered as dd/mm/yyyy hh:mm (for example 31/10/2026 18:00).
- Tick the acknowledgement box on the form, then press Submit.
- The application goes to your recommending authority and then your sanctioning authority for approval; their names are shown on the leave page (ask CampusAI for your leave balance to see them).
- Parents can be told about leave status and movement by SMS through the online leave system.
Source: UMS Hostel Leave Application page and Prospectus 2026 Part C

## Campus security and discipline rules
- 24x7 security with 200+ security staff and 7,000+ CCTV cameras; turnstile gates at main entry and exit points.
- Wear your identity card around the neck, visible, at all times on campus and on university trips.
- Strictly prohibited on and off campus: ragging, alcohol, smoking, drugs, gambling, weapons, fireworks, misconduct. Offenders face severe action and may be reported to police.
- Students may not drive inside the campus; park only in designated areas.
- No unauthorised gatherings, celebrations, posters, leaflets, fund collection or unions without written permission.
- Follow the uniform/dress code on prescribed days.
